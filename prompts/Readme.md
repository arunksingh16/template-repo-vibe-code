# Example prompts which i use everyday

> Review this as a senior engineer, but explain it to me as a technical product owner who is not a programmer. Go deep on the relevant implementation, but do not expand the scope.
>
> For every issue, explain:
>
> 1. What changed, in plain English.
> 2. Why it matters.
> 3. Whether it is a current bug, a future risk, or only cleanup.
> 4. What could happen to the user or service.
> 5. The smallest safe correction.
>
> Separate must-fix issues from optional improvements. Challenge unnecessary changes, preserve stable behaviour, and ask before expanding the ticket.

A shorter version is:

> Go deep, not broad. Explain this in plain English, distinguish real bugs from theoretical risks, and recommend the smallest safe change that preserves existing behaviour.

To get information:
> When reporting information to me, be extremely concise, sacrifice grammar for sake of concision. Be blunt and honest. I want a deeper version of this task, not a bigger one
